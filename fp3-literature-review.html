First Proof batch 3 · LOT with literature lemmas · Nov 2026

The paper was found. Why was the citation still rejected?论文找到了，为什么引用还是被拒？

In the fp3-lit experiment, LOT proof trees for First Proof problems P06, P08 and P09v2 may leave a lemma as sorry if it is a published human theorem. Each such lemma goes to an independent literature reviewer. Of 1,154 reviews, 240 were rejected, and in every one of them the reviewer did find the cited paper or book. This page explains what made those citations unusable, with real cases.

在 fp3-lit 实验里，First Proof 第 P06、P08、P09v2 题的 LOT 证明树可以把"已发表的人类定理"留作 sorry。每条这样的引理都要交给独立的文献审稿 agent 审核。1,154 次审核中有 240 次被拒，而这 240 次里审稿人每一次都找到了被引用的论文或书。本页说明这些引用为什么仍然不能用，并给出真实案例。

Short answer. The reviewers almost never doubted the mathematics: 131 of the 240 rejections say explicitly that they do not claim the Lean statement is false. They rejected the translation. In about 80% of rejections, the Lean lemma talks about the problem's own hand-built objects, a different definition, or a different category than the source. Bridging that gap is a theorem in its own right, and the rules forbid hiding a second theorem inside one citation.

一句话：审稿人几乎从不怀疑数学本身：240 次拒绝里有 131 次明确说"不是说这个 Lean 命题是假的"。他们拒绝的是翻译。约 80% 的拒绝里，Lean 引理讲的是题目自己手搭的对象、另一种定义，或另一个范畴（拓扑 vs 光滑/PL），而不是论文里的对象。把两者对上本身就是一条定理，而规则不允许把第二条定理藏进一次引用里。

What the reviewer checks审稿人检查什么

Each proposed literature lemma is reviewed by a separate codex session (gpt-6.1-sol, max effort, with web access). It reads the source itself and returns approved or rejected with a reason, the source URL and a verbatim quote. A lemma is approved only if all four checks pass:

每条文献引理由一个独立的 codex session（gpt-6.1-sol，max effort，可联网）审核。它必须亲自读原文，输出 approved 或 rejected，附理由、原文链接和逐字引文。四条检查全部通过才算通过：

1 · EXISTSThe cited theorem exists under the cited number, read in the source itself.被引定理确实以所引编号存在，并且是在原文中亲自读到的。
2 · ONE THEOREMOne source, one numbered result, or a direct, routine reformulation of it. A chain of results, or a theorem plus a non-trivial argument, is rejected.一个出处、一条编号结果，或它直接、常规的改写。多个结果串起来，或"定理 + 非平凡论证"，都拒绝。
3 · IMPLIES THE LEANUnder the problem's own ChallengeDeps.lean definitions the source implies the Lean statement: no missing hypotheses, no stronger conclusion, the hand-made objects are the source's objects, degenerate cases hold.在题目自己的 ChallengeDeps.lean 定义下，原文能推出这个 Lean 命题：不缺假设、结论不更强、手搭对象就是原文的对象、退化情形也成立。
4 · NOT THE PROBLEMThe lemma is not the problem itself (this never triggered).引理不能就是题目本身（这一条从未触发）。

Check 2 is the one that bites: 227 of the 240 rejection reasons invoke the one-theorem / "routine reformulation" rule. 226 of them also propose an acceptable repair.

真正卡人的是第 2 条：240 条拒绝理由里有 227 条援引了"一条定理 / 常规改写"规则。其中 226 条同时给出了可接受的修改建议。

What fixed them怎么修好的

24 lemma names (9 in P06, 2 in P08, 13 in P09v2) were rejected and later approved in some form. The repairs were almost always local:

有 24 个引理名（P06 9 个、P08 2 个、P09v2 13 个）先被拒、后来以某种形式通过。修法几乎都是局部的：

  • Fix the number: cite the theorem that actually says it (case 5).
  • Add the hypothesis hidden in the source's definitions: connectedness for Lee's covering maps (case 4).
  • Fix the Lean encoding: ⊤ → (↑⊤), so Cω becomes C∞ (case 3).
  • Switch to a source in the right setting: abstract rather than embedded manifolds, topological rather than smooth (cases 6, 7, 11).
  • Restate in the source's own definitions: e.g. quasiconformality in the W1,2 form that Koskela–Wildrick prove (case 10).
  • 改编号：引用真正说这件事的那条定理（案例 5）。
  • 补上藏在原文定义里的假设：例如 Lee 的覆叠映射要求连通（案例 4）。
  • 修 Lean 写法：⊤ → (↑⊤)，即 Cω 改为 C∞（案例 3）。
  • 换一个设定匹配的出处：抽象流形而非嵌入流形、拓扑范畴而非光滑范畴（案例 6、7、11）。
  • 改用原文自己的定义来陈述：例如按 Koskela–Wildrick 证明的 W1,2 形式表述拟共形（案例 10）。

What could not be fixed locally is the bespoke-object gap (cases 8, 9). Equating a grid/polygon model with genuine HF-hat, or a hand-made quotient MBar with (M̄g)an, is a research-level comparison theorem. No single citation covers it, so the tree has to prove that bridge or route around it.

无法局部修好的是"手搭对象"这一类（案例 8、9）。证明网格/多边形模型等于真正的 HF-hat，或手搭的商空间 MBar 等于 (M̄g)an，本身就是研究级的比较定理。没有哪一次引用能覆盖它，证明树只能自己证明这座桥，或者绕开它。

Other observations其他观察

  • The reviewer is not deterministic. In case 2 the same elaborated statement, byte for byte, was rejected at 11:30Z and approved at 12:19Z. irreducible_prime was approved once and rejected five times. Strictness about the smooth-vs-topological category varies between sessions.
  • Only one convention gap was waived, and it took a human. P08's final proof rests on Forni Thm 3.10, approved under an explicit operator ruling (case 1). Everything else in that ruling was still checked as usual.
  • The reviewers found real bugs in the problem's own docstrings. Four independent P09v2 reviews built the same counterexample (the radial map exp(−1/|z|²)·z/|z|). It refutes a docstring's claim that the problem's chart-dependent circular dilatation is invariant under conformal chart changes.
  • Read the elaborated statement, not the comment. P08's frechet_open_mapping was rejected because what Lean elaborated was stronger than what LEMMA.md said. The written version would have passed.
  • Paywalls count as rejections. Five rejections came from full texts the reviewer could not open (case 13). The rules require reading the theorem in the source.
  • 审稿结果不是确定性的。案例 2 里，逐字节相同的同一个 elaborated 命题 11:30Z 被拒、12:19Z 被通过。irreducible_prime 通过 1 次、被拒 5 次。不同 session 对"光滑 vs 拓扑范畴"的严格程度不一样。
  • 只有一个约定差异被豁免，而且需要人来拍板。P08 的最终证明依赖 Forni Thm 3.10，是在一条明确的人工裁定下通过的（案例 1）。裁定之外的部分照常检查。
  • 审稿人发现了题目自身 docstring 的真实错误。P09v2 有 4 次独立审核构造出了同一个反例（径向映射 exp(−1/|z|²)·z/|z|）。它否定了 docstring 里"依赖坐标卡的圆周伸缩在共形换卡下不变"的说法。
  • 要看 elaborate 之后的命题，不能只看注释。P08 的 frechet_open_mapping 被拒，是因为 Lean 实际 elaborate 出的命题比 LEMMA.md 里写的更强。按写的版本本来能过。
  • 付费墙也会导致被拒。有 5 次拒绝是因为打不开全文（案例 13）。规则要求必须在原文里读到定理。
