Parallel Reasoning LLMs — Parason Jul 2025 – Jun 2026

Leading Author, arXiv:2608.24658
Advisor: Ligeng Zhu, Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA

  • Curated a parallel SFT dataset from S-1 reasoning datasets, achieving better performance than sequential SFT with lower latency and building the state-of-the-art parallel reasoning model.
  • Divided parallel reasoning into two types — subtask parallelism and trial parallelism — representing collective and competitive parallelism respectively.
  • Designed novel reward functions for RL that boost trial parallelism, reaching similar accuracy with only 1/4 of the original thinking token budget.
  • Conducted extensive ablation studies on how different parallelism types affect the latency and accuracy of the reasoning process.
