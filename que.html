The strict closed-orbit mass bound, a few steps further

The original theorem says that on a compact hyperbolic surface, a semiclassical measure of Laplace eigenfunctions puts strictly less than half of its mass on a closed geodesic. On top of its completed Lean formalization we proved three extensions: the constant does not depend on the surface (S0), the bound holds for approximate eigenfunctions (S1), and it holds for the union of finitely many closed geodesics (S2). This page explains what each one adds.

The original result

Let M be a compact connected orientable surface of curvature −1, γ a primitive closed geodesic, and Γγ the union of its two oriented lifts to the unit cosphere bundle S*M. Theorem A: there is ε > 0 such that every semiclassical limit μ of every sequence of normalized Laplace eigenfunctions satisfies

μ(Γγ) ≤ ½ − ε

No Hecke, reality, simple-spectrum or subsequence condition is imposed. The bound known before was μ(Γγ) ≤ ½ (the entropy bound of Anantharaman–Nonnenmacher, 2007), and the measure ½·Liouville + ½·(the measure on γ) sits exactly on it; the full-support theorems do not exclude it either. Theorem A is the first to exclude this "half scar".

Theorem A is fully formalized in Lean 4: 459 files, about 159,000 lines, no sorry. The proof depends only on the 3 standard axioms and 27 "black boxes", each a verbatim translation of one published result (arXiv preprints included), checked item by item against its source.

The three extensions strengthen Theorem A in three different directions: S0 strengthens the quantifier (a more uniform constant), S1 enlarges the class of states (a more robust conclusion), and S2 enlarges the set being measured (more counterexamples excluded). None of them pushes the bound ½ − ε lower; bringing the closed-orbit mass down to 0 is still an open problem.

S0: one ε for every surface

In the original theorem, ε is given after the surface and the closed geodesic are chosen; read literally, every surface may have its own ε. S0 proves that a universal ε exists, valid for every compact hyperbolic surface and every closed geodesic at once.

What it adds to the original

The two statements differ in one place: where ∃ ε stands.

-- Theorem A (original)
∀ M g, (curvature −1) → ∀ γ, (primitive closed geodesic) → ∃ ε > 0, ∀ Q u μ, … → μ(Γ_γ) ≤ ½ − ε

-- S0
∃ ε > 0, ∀ M g, (curvature −1) → ∀ γ, (primitive closed geodesic) → ∀ Q u μ, … → μ(Γ_γ) ≤ ½ − ε
  • Before: every surface and every closed geodesic has its own ε. In principle, the ε of a sequence of ever more complicated surfaces could tend to 0.
  • Now: the same ε works whatever the genus, however distorted the surface, however long or short the closed geodesic.

This is exactly what Remark 1.2 of the manuscript claims and what the original formalization did not prove.

What it reveals

The proof only needs a rearrangement because ε comes from two things that do not see the geometry:

  • the exponent β of the Cohen–Dyatlov nonlinear uncertainty principle;
  • a purely combinatorial selection theorem (Theorem 2.18 of the paper).

The geometry of the surface (curvature, length of the closed orbit, injectivity radius) only enters constants in the middle of the proof, and all of them disappear in the limits. So ε can only be improved by improving the uncertainty exponent or the combinatorial selection theorem; better geometric estimates do not help.

The proof

The main proof chain only passes the statement down level by level; the surface M is introduced only at the bottom level, and ε is fixed by the two steps above before that. The proof therefore does two things:

  1. universal_of_nodes: move the choice of ε in front of "for every surface";
  2. window_reduction: assemble the window-reduction statement the proof needs directly from nodes that are already proved.

No new mathematics and no new axioms.

What it does not show

  • The numerical value of ε is still unknown: it comes from a compactness argument and a non-explicit exponent.
  • It says nothing about limits of sequences of surfaces at fixed frequency (such as genus tending to infinity): each surface in the theorem is fixed while the frequency tends to infinity.
  • The bound is still ½ − ε; it did not get smaller.
S1 · proved · 2026-11-23

S1: the frequency need not be exact

The original theorem requires genuine Laplace eigenfunctions, with no error in the frequency at all. S1 proves that as long as the frequency is blurred by less than h/log(1/h), the conclusion μ(Γγ) ≤ ½ − ε still holds.

What it adds to the original

One hypothesis changes: "exact eigenfunction" becomes "logarithmic quasimode".

-- Theorem A (original): exact eigenfunctions
Δ_g u_j = −λ_j² u_j

-- S1: logarithmic quasimodes (h = 1/λ)
‖Δ_g u_j + λ_j² u_j‖ ≤ δ_j · λ_j / log λ_j,   δ_j → 0
-- equivalently: ‖(−h²Δ − 1) u‖ = o(h / log(1/h))

Exact eigenfunctions are the case δ = 0, so S1 is strictly stronger than Theorem A. ε is quantified as before: chosen after the surface and the closed geodesic, then valid for all quasimode sequences at once.

Why exactly h/log(1/h)

Spectral width (frequency blur)Closed-orbit mass
0 (exact eigenfunctions)≤ ½ − ε (Theorem A)
o(h/log(1/h))≤ ½ − ε (S1)
c·h/log(1/h), c fixedcan be > 0: there are examples that scar a hyperbolic closed orbit (Eswarathasan–Nonnenmacher, 2017)

Every tool in the proof (Egorov's theorem, the uncertainty principle, the occupation argument) follows the dynamics only up to times of order log(1/h), the Ehrenfest time. Over that time, the accumulated difference between a state of width o(h/log(1/h)) and an exact eigenfunction still tends to 0; a little wider and one meets counterexamples that really exist. S1 thus pins the limit of the present method at exactly h/log(1/h).

Why it matters

  • The conclusion is robust: it does not rely on the idealized condition that the frequency is exact.
  • It is the foundation of any "iteration" scheme. To push the closed-orbit mass down step by step, every step (for example, cutting out the part near the orbit and filtering the frequency again) produces a state that is no longer an exact eigenfunction but a quasimode of narrow width. Without S1 the theorem could not be applied at the second step.

The structure of the proof

In the original proof, exactness of the eigenfunction is used in only three places. The tree split the root accordingly:

  1. Spectral control: turn the quasimode condition into an estimate on the spectral expansion, giving the approximate phase of the propagator ‖U(t)u − e−it/hu‖ = o(|t|/log(1/h));
  2. Fixed-time nodes: quasimode versions of support invariance and of the fixed-time limits;
  3. Logarithmic-time capture: replace the time-shift invariance of the observation statistics by an approximate version whose error, summed over logarithmically many steps, is o(1);
  4. Assembly: the new error disappears in the first limit (j → ∞); the later steps are unchanged, and so is ε.

No new mathematical idea is needed; the difficulty is technical. The spectral lemma, first seen as the riskiest step, was the first to be proved.

What it does not show

  • The bound is still ½ − ε; it did not get smaller.
  • It does not give the "gain lemma" (that each step can push the scar a little higher). Given S1, the gain lemma is equivalent to "the closed-orbit mass is 0", which is still an open problem.

S2: finitely many closed geodesics together also stay below ½ − ε

The original theorem covers one closed geodesic. S2 proves that for any finitely many closed geodesics γ1, …, γk, the total mass a semiclassical measure puts on the union of their lifts is also at most ½ − ε.

What it adds to the original

-- Theorem A (original): one closed geodesic
∀ γ, (primitive closed geodesic) → ∃ ε > 0, … → μ(Γ_γ) ≤ ½ − ε

-- S2: any finitely many closed geodesics
∀ k, ∀ γ₁ … γ_k, (each a primitive closed geodesic) → ∃ ε > 0, … → μ(Γ_γ₁ ∪ … ∪ Γ_γk) ≤ ½ − ε

It excludes "spread-out half scars" that the original theorem does not reach, for example

½·Liouville + ¼·(the measure on γ1) + ¼·(the measure on γ2)

This measure puts only ¼ on each closed geodesic, so the original theorem does not object; but the two together carry ½, exactly the old threshold, and it is the same kind of object as the half scar that Theorem A excludes. It is the most natural question to ask after reading Theorem A, and S2 answers it directly.

Why it should hold

  • The abstract part is unaffected. The operator-algebra part of the proof only distinguishes "inside the tube" from "outside". Replacing the tube around one closed geodesic by a tube around a finite union changes nothing there.
  • The tubes do not overlap. Where two distinct closed geodesics cross on the surface their directions differ, so their lifts to S*M are disjoint; thin enough tubes are disjoint.
  • The geometric part has to change. The original proof counts the translates of one "axis" (the group element of the closed geodesic) on the boundary of the hyperbolic plane. Now there are k axes: each visit picks one, and the counting constant gains a factor k. That constant disappears in the limits, so ε is not affected.

What it does not show

  • The bound is still ½ − ε; it did not get smaller.
  • Only finitely many. There are countably many closed geodesics and their union is dense in S*M; S2 says nothing about it.
  • It does not exclude mass concentrating on a fractal invariant set that contains no single closed orbit; that needs a different extension (for example, hyperbolic sets of small entropy).

严格闭轨质量界：在原定理之上又往前走了几步

原定理说：在紧致双曲曲面上，Laplace 本征函数的半经典测度放在一条闭测地线上的质量严格小于一半。在它已经完成的 Lean 形式化之上，我们又证明了三个推广：常数与曲面无关（S0）、对近似本征函数也成立（S1）、对有限条闭测地线的并也成立（S2）。本页说明它们分别比原来多了什么。

原来的结果

设 M 是紧致、连通、可定向、曲率恒为 −1 的曲面，γ 是一条本原闭测地线，Γγ 是它在单位余球丛 S*M 上的两个定向提升。定理 A：存在 ε > 0，使得任意归一化 Laplace 本征函数序列的任意半经典极限 μ 都满足

μ(Γγ) ≤ ½ − ε

不需要 Hecke、实值、单谱或子序列条件。此前已知的界是 μ(Γγ) ≤ ½（Anantharaman–Nonnenmacher 2007 的熵下界），而测度 ½·Liouville + ½·(γ 上的测度) 正好卡在这个界上，满支撑定理也排除不了它。定理 A 第一次排除了这种"半疤痕"。

定理 A 已在 Lean 4 中完整形式化：459 个文件、约 15.9 万行，没有 sorry。证明只依赖 3 个标准公理和 27 个"黑箱"，每个黑箱都是一条已发表结果（包括 arXiv 预印本）的逐字翻译，并逐条对照原文审核过。

它揭示了什么

这个证明之所以只需要重新排列，是因为 ε 只来自两个与几何无关的东西：

  • Cohen–Dyatlov 非线性不确定性原理给出的指数 β；
  • 一个纯组合的选择定理（论文定理 2.18）。

曲面的几何（曲率、闭轨长度、单射半径）只影响证明中途的常数，这些常数在取极限时全部消失。所以要改进 ε，只能改进不确定性原理的指数或那个组合选择定理；改进几何估计没有用。

证明

主定理的证明链只是把陈述一层层往下传，曲面 M 只在最底层才被引入，而 ε 在那一层之前就已经由上面两步确定了。所以证明只做了两件事：

  1. universal_of_nodes：把 ε 的选取挪到"任取曲面"之前；
  2. window_reduction：直接用已有的已证节点组装出证明所需的窗口约化陈述。

没有新的数学，也没有新的公理。

它不说明什么

  • ε 的具体数值仍然不知道：它来自紧性论证和一个非显式的指数。
  • 它管不到"曲面序列、频率固定"那类极限（比如亏格趋于无穷），定理里每个曲面都是固定的，再让频率趋于无穷。
  • 上界还是 ½ − ε，没有变小。

和原来比，进步在哪里

只改了一个假设：把"精确本征函数"换成"对数拟模"。

-- 定理 A（原来）：精确本征函数
Δ_g u_j = −λ_j² u_j

-- S1：对数拟模（h = 1/λ）
‖Δ_g u_j + λ_j² u_j‖ ≤ δ_j · λ_j / log λ_j,   δ_j → 0
-- 等价地：‖(−h²Δ − 1) u‖ = o(h / log(1/h))

精确本征函数是 δ = 0 的特例，所以 S1 严格强于定理 A。ε 和原来一样：先选曲面和闭测地线，再对所有拟模序列一起成立。

为什么正好是 h/log(1/h)

谱宽（频率模糊度）闭轨质量
0（精确本征函数）≤ ½ − ε（定理 A）
o(h/log(1/h))≤ ½ − ε（S1）

证明里用到的所有工具（Egorov 定理、不确定性原理、占据论证）只追踪到 log(1/h) 量级的时间（Ehrenfest 时间）。在这个时间内，谱宽 o(h/log(1/h)) 的状态和精确本征函数的误差累积起来仍然趋于 0；再宽一点就会碰到真实存在的反例。S1 因此把现有方法的边界精确地定在 h/log(1/h)。

为什么它重要

  • 结论是稳健的，不依赖"频率完全精确"这个理想条件。
  • 它是任何"迭代"方案的地基。想把闭轨质量一步步压到 0，每一步操作（比如截取闭轨附近的部分再过滤频率）产生的新状态都不再是精确本征函数，而是窄谱宽的拟模。没有 S1，定理在迭代的第二步就用不上了。

证明的结构

在原证明里，"精确本征"只在三个地方用到。树把根节点拆成了对应的几块：

  1. 谱控制：把拟模条件转成谱展开里的估计，得到传播子的近似相位 ‖U(t)u − e−it/hu‖ = o(|t|/log(1/h))；
  2. 固定时间节点：支撑不变性和固定时间极限的拟模版本；
  3. 对数时间捕获：把"观测统计的时间平移不变性"换成近似版本，误差在对数步数内累积为 o(1)；
  4. 组装：新误差在第一个极限（j → ∞）里就消失，后面的步骤不变，ε 也不变。

不需要新的数学想法，难点是技术细节；最初认为风险最大的谱引理最先被证完。

它不说明什么

  • 上界还是 ½ − ε，没有变小。
  • 它不能推出"增益引理"（"每一步能把疤痕再推高一点"）。在 S1 成立的前提下，增益引理等价于"闭轨质量为 0"，仍是公开问题。

为什么应该成立

  • 抽象部分不受影响。证明的算子代数部分只区分"在管子里"和"在管子外"。把一条闭测地线的管子换成有限条的并，这部分一个字都不用改。

半经典输入对数时间尺度上的标签观测量：测度在能量壳上且不变、块内交换子、一点和相邻两点的一致极限已证明
组装从有限占据估计到质量上界，再到根节点已证明

它不说明什么

  • 上界还是 ½ − ε，没有变小。
  • 只管有限条。所有闭测地线有可数无穷多条，它们的并在 S*M 中稠密，S2 不涉及。
  • 它不排除能量集中在不含单独闭轨的分形不变集上的情形；那需要别的推广（例如小熵双曲集）。

怎么验证的

  • 每个推广都写成一个 Lean 定理陈述，import 整个已有的形式化（Mathlib c55e6e786f49、DifferentialGeometry、QUE），用 PaperLean v0.2.4 工具链编译。
  • 证明由 LOT（Lean Option Tree）自动拆分、并行求证：一棵树对应一个定理，每个子目标都是一个冻结的 Lean 陈述，必须单独通过检查。
  • 最终证明由同一个检查器验收：comparator 核对陈述没有被改动，nanoda（独立于 Lean 的内核）重放整个证明。允许的公理只有 3 个标准公理和已有形式化里审核过的 35 个黑箱；不允许 sorry，不允许新公理。
  • 检查器做过端到端测试：用原定理的真实证明提交，被接受；用 sorry 提交，被拒绝。
